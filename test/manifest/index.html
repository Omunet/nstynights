Proposed

Manifest

A working text for the night. It sets a tone. It does not open a door.

After the lights drop

The good hour is the one where the room gets quieter in the head and louder in the body. People arrive already decided: latex, tailoring, or something darker and simpler. The point is to be present, not to be explained.

Material before myth

Latex is a surface. It takes fingerprints of light and keeps a shape. Around it: hardware, feather, a clean black line. NSTY is that palette, stretched into a night. The clothing is made in Barcelona. The night itself has not been given a room yet.

The floor stays unfinished

Until a date is printed, there is no guest list hiding in the silence. A manifesto can want a room. It cannot pretend the room is already booked.
