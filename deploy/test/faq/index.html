FAQ

These replies describe the preview. They are not an approved house policy.

Is a night announced?

No. This preview does not publish a date, a venue, or a lineup.

Can I buy a ticket?

No sales link is published. There is no checkout on this site.

How do I reach you?

The only verified destination is the Instagram profile @lx.nsty. Its public text is about latex clothing and direct messages for orders. This preview has no form and no email address. The contact page holds the link.

Is member access open?

No. The access page explains that no login exists yet.

What is the age limit?

TBD. This draft does not state one.

What should I wear?

See the dress-code draft. It is not an entry requirement.

Which photographs are shown?

Only files supplied from posts authored or co-authored by @lx.nsty. Posts where the account is only tagged stay out. A house rule for cameras inside a night is TBD.

Are older posters a list of upcoming nights?

No. The 2025 poster is in the dated archive, with the lines it prints. It is not a future schedule.

Do you set cookies?

This preview does not set cookies and does not load analytics or remote fonts.

Is the wording final?

No. Manifesto, rules, dress notes, questions, and the legal page are drafts awaiting approval.
