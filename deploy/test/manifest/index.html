Manifest

A proposal for the tone of NSTY nights. It is not a promise of an event.

01

A night, not a counter

NSTY on this domain names a night that has not been announced. The Instagram account beside it is public as a Barcelona latex clothing practice that takes orders by direct message. Sharing a name does not turn this website into a catalogue. Garments are not listed here. Nights are not scheduled here.

02

Silence is not a guest list

An empty date, an empty room, and an empty ticket line mean those facts are not published. They do not mean a guest list is open, a room is held, or a lineup is settled. When a night is real, it will be stated with a date and a place. Until then, a blank is a blank.

03

Conduct before costume

A dress proposal can sit on its own page without being a rule of entry. How people treat one another will matter more than what they wear, and the house notes for that are still a draft. This preview does not invent an age limit, a search policy, or a right of admission and then pretend it is already in force.
