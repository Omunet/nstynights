Proposed

Dress code

A direction, not a rule of entry. Wear the night, or wear black and mean it.

Latex

The house color is shine: black, brown, and the hard highlight along a seam. Gloves and a collar belong in the same sentence.

Backstage black

Dark latex, a mask, the corridor before the light. Useful if you want the night without a costume speech.

Pale latex

White with a black graphic, a long drape, a boot with height. The mark in that photograph belongs to the event that printed it.

Fur and hardware

A coat with weight, a harness, a collar. Texture against shine. Plain dark clothes are welcome too. This page does not turn anyone away.
